TRUST & COVERAGE

Data methodology

✓ No estimates
01
Statutory land valuations

Median of eligible NSW Valuer General assessed parcel land values within each suburb, by valuation year. Excludes buildings and improvements.

02
Actual sold house transactions

Median of verified eligible house sale prices, by suburb, period and bedroom band where bedrooms are supplied by a licensed dataset. No bedroom inference.

03
Clear data provenance

Every published metric requires a source, period, number of observations and coverage notes. Missing data remain explicitly unavailable.
